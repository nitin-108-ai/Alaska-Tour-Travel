Transport & Ticket Booking Guide

Native booking portals & steps for Flights, Indian Railways Trains, and Intercity Buses across India.

1. How to Book Flight Tickets

  1. Select departure & arrival airports with desired travel date.
  2. Compare fares across top airlines (IndiGo, Air India, Alaska Airlines, Akasa).
  3. Fill passenger details as per Government ID (Aadhaar / Passport).
  4. Pay via UPI or Cards to receive instant confirmed PNR & E-Ticket.
Search Flights View All Flights

2. How to Book Train Tickets (Indian Railways)

  1. Enter your Source and Destination stations (e.g. Mumbai CSMT to Delhi NDLS, Goa, Varanasi).
  2. Browse Vande Bharat, Rajdhani Express, Shatabdi, and Superfast express trains.
  3. Choose class (1A First AC, 2A Tier, 3A Tier, CC Chair Car, SL Sleeper) with live seat availability.
  4. Complete booking to receive confirmed IRCTC PNR & E-Ticket saved in your profile.
Search & Book Trains (Live Status)

3. How to Book Bus Tickets (Inter-City & Volvo)

  1. Choose origin and destination cities (e.g. Delhi, Manali, Jaipur, Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, Goa).
  2. Filter by Volvo Multi-Axle, AC Sleeper, Semi-Sleeper, or Electric buses.
  3. Pick your preferred lower or upper deck berth using the interactive seat layout.
  4. Confirm booking to receive instant M-Ticket and invoice.
