Terms & Conditions

Alaska Tour & Travel provides travel-related services including flight booking, hotel booking, bus booking, car rental and tour packages.

  • Users are responsible for checking their booking details before confirmation.

  • Misuse of the website or fraudulent booking activity is not permitted.
02

Booking Policy

Information about reservations

When you submit a booking request through our website, you must provide correct travel and passenger information.

  • Booking requests are subject to availability.
  • A booking is considered confirmed only after confirmation from Alaska Tour & Travel.
  • Incorrect passenger information may result in additional charges or cancellation.
  • Prices and availability may change before final confirmation.
03

Payment Policy

Payment and pricing information

Payment requirements may vary depending on the selected travel service, airline, hotel or tour package.

  • All applicable prices will be displayed before final booking confirmation.
  • Taxes, service charges or additional fees may apply.
  • Customers should keep their payment confirmation for future reference.
  • We do not request sensitive payment information through unofficial channels.
04

Cancellation Policy

Cancelling or changing your booking

Cancellation and modification rules depend on the service provider and the type of booking.

  • Cancellation requests should be submitted as early as possible.
  • Airline, hotel or transportation providers may apply their own cancellation charges.
  • Some promotional or discounted bookings may be non-refundable.
  • Changes to travel dates may be subject to availability and additional charges.
05

Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

We respect your privacy and aim to protect the information you provide while using our website.

  • We may collect information such as your name, contact details and travel preferences when you submit a booking request.
  • Information may be used to process bookings and provide customer support.
  • We do not intentionally sell personal information to third parties.
  • Information may be shared with relevant service providers when required to complete a booking.
  • Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through public forms.
06

Refund Policy

Refund eligibility and processing

Refund eligibility depends on the rules of the airline, hotel, transportation provider or tour package selected.

  • Eligible refunds will be processed according to the applicable booking conditions.
  • Service fees may not be refundable.
  • Refund processing times may vary depending on the payment provider.
  • Promotional bookings may have different refund conditions.
